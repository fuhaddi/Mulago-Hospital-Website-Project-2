About the Hospital

A Century of Service to the Nation

Situated on Mulago Hill in Kampala, Mulago National Referral Hospital stands as the pinnacle of public healthcare in Uganda. Founded in 1913, the hospital has grown from a modest colonial medical facility into a sprawling, modern institution that serves as the country's primary center for specialist care and medical training.

As the national referral hospital, Mulago receives patients from every district across Uganda and from neighboring countries. It is closely affiliated with Makerere University College of Health Sciences, making it one of the leading teaching hospitals in East Africa.

What We Offer

Key Services

We offer a comprehensive range of medical services delivered by skilled, dedicated professionals committed to the health and well-being of every patient.

Emergency and Casualty

Our 24-hour emergency department is equipped to handle trauma, acute illness, and life-threatening conditions at any time of day or night.

Maternity and Obstetrics

We provide comprehensive maternal care, including antenatal services, skilled birth attendance, postnatal care, and high-risk pregnancy management.

Surgical Services

Our surgical suites handle general, orthopaedic, cardiothoracic, and neurosurgical procedures, supported by a dedicated anaesthesia team.

Paediatrics

Specialised care for infants, children, and adolescents, including the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) and a dedicated children's ward.

Diagnostics and Laboratory

State-of-the-art laboratory, radiology, and imaging services to support accurate diagnosis and effective treatment planning.

Outpatient Department

Our outpatient clinics cover a wide range of specialties, allowing patients to consult with specialists without hospital admission.
