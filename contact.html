Find Us

Our Location on the Map

Mulago National Referral Hospital is located on Mulago Hill, approximately 3 kilometres north of Kampala city centre, easily accessible by all modes of transport.

🚘

By Private Car

From Kampala city centre, take Bombo Road north. Turn right onto Mulago Hill Road and follow signs to the hospital main entrance. Free parking is available on site.

🚌

By Taxi / Matatu

Board any taxi from Kampala Old Taxi Park heading to Mulago. Ask the conductor to drop you at Mulago Hospital. The journey takes about 15 to 20 minutes from the city centre.

🚲

By Boda-Boda

Boda-bodas (motorcycle taxis) are available throughout Kampala. Simply ask to be taken to Mulago Hospital on Mulago Hill. They are a fast option during peak hours.

🚨

Ambulance Transfer

For patients being transferred from other health facilities by ambulance, the main hospital ambulance bay is located at the Emergency and Casualty entrance on the lower side of the hill.
